Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This is placeholder content for the demo website. Final wording should be confirmed by Ma Fullers South African Pantry before going live.

1. Our commitment

Ma Fullers South African Pantry respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle any personal information you share with us, in line with the Privacy Act 2020 (New Zealand).

2. Information we collect

As a local shop, we collect very little. We may collect your information when you:

  • Contact us by phone or email about an order, hamper or enquiry.
  • Place a special order or request a product be set aside.
  • Choose to leave a review or reach out via our social media pages.

This may include your name, phone number, email address and the details of your enquiry.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to respond to you, fulfil orders, and keep you updated about a request you've made. We don't sell or trade your personal information to anyone.

4. Payments

In-store payments are handled through our EFTPOS and card providers. We do not store your full card details.

5. Third-party services

This website uses Google Maps to show our location and links to third-party platforms such as Google and Facebook. Those services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read.

6. Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect any personal information we hold and to keep it only as long as we need it.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request corrections. Just get in touch and we'll help.

8. Contact us

For any privacy questions, pop in to 174 Williams Street, Kaiapoi, or call +64 21 025 70925.
