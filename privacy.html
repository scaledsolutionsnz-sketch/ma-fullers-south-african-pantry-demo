Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Ma Fullers South African Pantry handles personal information in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. This is a placeholder policy for this demo site and will be replaced with the shop's own policy before launch.

What we collect

If you call, email or place a special order, we may keep your name, phone number, email address and the details of your order. We only collect what you choose to give us.

How we use it

We use it to answer your questions, prepare orders and hampers, and let you know when they're ready. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Raffles and competitions

If you enter an in-store raffle, we use your details to contact you if you win. With your permission we may share the winner's first name on our Facebook page.

This website

This site has no forms and does not set its own tracking cookies. The embedded Google Map is provided by Google, and its privacy policy applies when you use it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by calling 0210 257 0925.
