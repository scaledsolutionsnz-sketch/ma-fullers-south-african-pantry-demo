Our story

The South African pantry Kaiapoi didn't know it needed

Ma Fullers started with a simple idea — that no one in North Canterbury should have to go without their biltong, their Ouma rusks or a proper boerewors braai. So we opened up on Williams Street and filled the shelves with the flavours of home.

Run by Beth and the team, it's more than a shop. It's the place where South Africans in Kaiapoi swap stories, kiwis discover something new, and everyone leaves with a bag of something delicious.

  • ✓ Locally owned and run, right here in Kaiapoi
  • ✓ Fresh biltong & boerewors, plus hard-to-find SA brands
  • ✓ Friendly advice — we'll help you find exactly what you're after
