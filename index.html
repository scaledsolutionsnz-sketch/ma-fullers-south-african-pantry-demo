Our story

A little piece of home on Williams Street

Ma Fullers is a family-run South African shop in the middle of Kaiapoi. The biltong and droëwors are made in-store, vetkoek and boerewors rolls come fresh from the counter, and the shelves are full of the brands South Africans miss.

South Africans come in for a taste of home, Kiwis come in curious, and everyone gets a chat and a taste before they choose. In 2026 Kaiapoi Promotions named it Shop Kaiapoi's Best Business.

FAQ

Before you pop in

Anything else, give the shop a ring on 0210 257 0925.

Where are you and when are you open?

174 Williams Street, Kaiapoi. We're open 7 days: Monday 11:30am–5:30pm, Tuesday to Friday 9:30am–5:30pm, Saturday 9:30am–4pm and Sunday 10am–2pm.

Can I get something to eat?

Yes. The counter does fresh food like breakfast vetkoek, filled vetkoek, boerewors rolls and jaffles. Ask what's on today when you come in.

Can I taste the biltong first?

Please do. The biltong and droëwors are made in the shop, and we're happy to let you try before you choose.

Can you check if something's in stock?

Give us a call before you make the trip and we'll have a look on the shelves for you.

Do you make up gift hampers?

We've put together hampers of South African treats before. Ring the shop to talk about what you'd like in one.

Do you courier orders?

Ask us. Call or email with what you're after and where you are, and we'll let you know what we can do.
