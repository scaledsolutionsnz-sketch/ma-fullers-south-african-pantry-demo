From the counter

Something proper to eat while you shop

Come in hungry. These are made fresh at the counter, the South African way.

  • Bunny chow

    A quarter loaf of homemade bread filled with lamb curry, served with sambals and chutney.

  • Breakfast vetkoek

    The South African breakfast favourite, filled with bacon, egg and cheese and covered in homemade relish.

  • Vetkoek

    Filled with lamb curry, beef curry or savoury mince, sweet with Nutella, or plain.

  • Boerewors rolls

    With fried onions and tomato relish, or tomato sauce and mustard.

  • Jaffles

    South African toasted sandwiches with lamb curry, beef curry, savoury mince, or cheese, red onion and tomato with chutney.

Prices and today's fillings are on the boards at the counter.

Our story

A little piece of home on Williams Street

Ma Fullers is a family owned and run South African shop in the middle of Kaiapoi. Beth and her family make the biltong and droëwors in the shop, cook vetkoek, bunny chow and boerewors rolls at the counter, and keep the shelves full of the brands South Africans miss.

South Africans come in for a taste of home, Kiwis come in curious, and everyone gets a friendly chat and a taste before they choose. In 2026 Kaiapoi Promotions named Ma Fullers the Shop Kaiapoi Best Business.

FAQ

Before you pop in

Anything else, give the shop a ring on 0210 257 0925.

Where are you and when are you open?

174 Williams Street, Kaiapoi. We're open 7 days: Monday 11:30am to 5:30pm, Tuesday to Friday 9:30am to 5:30pm, Saturday 9:30am to 4pm and Sunday 10am to 2pm.

Can I get something to eat?

Yes. The counter does bunny chow, breakfast vetkoek, filled vetkoek, boerewors rolls and jaffles. Ask what's on today when you come in.

What biltong flavours do you have?

The cabinet usually has flavours like chilli, spicy, safari, chutney, chakalaka, babelas and salt and vinegar, plus chilli sticks, blades and bacon. They change, so ask what's in today.

Can I taste the biltong first?

Please do. The biltong and droëwors are made in the shop, and we're happy to let you try before you choose.

Can you check if something's in stock?

Give us a call before you make the trip and we'll have a look on the shelves for you.

Do you make up gift hampers?

We've put together hampers of South African treats before. Ring the shop to talk about what you'd like in one.
